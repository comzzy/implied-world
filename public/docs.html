What it is

Implied World is an overnight rToken research desk for NVDA, TSLA, and AAPL. It freezes tagged market inputs, draws an implied gap band into the next US cash open, runs stress and twin checks, and can draft a short write-up from that frozen table. It does not place orders. You decide.

Why Implied World

Tokenized equity wrappers trade when the US cash book is closed. Premium can look rich or thin overnight without a clear map of how much room remains into Monday’s open, once BTC residual and the calendar are in the picture. The desk exists so those pieces sit in one place, labelled, with the math auditable — not as a trade ticket.

The problem it solves

A weekend thesis often mixes live wrapper premium, a cash last print, a residual BTC move, and a guess at event weight. Without tags, failed feeds look like calm quotes. Without a band, “still has room” is a feeling. Without a counter-thesis and a first-to-break list, the desk only tells one story. Implied World freezes the inputs you have, marks what is missing, solves the band, and leaves the call with you.

Desk

Pick a symbol, thesis, style (weekend_swing, event_window, or intraday_wrapper), and notional. Run desk freezes cash last, wrapper / rToken premium, BTC residual, event weight, and hours to cash open. Each line carries a data-quality tag.

  • Band — implied gap mid = premium + k × event × residual BTC, with lo/hi from a fixed stress pad. Status is room left, no room, or open but unstable — never a side.
  • Lamp — ALIGNED or CONTESTED from how this thesis sits against the counter-thesis and factor check.
  • Briefing — a write-up from the frozen table only. If the writer is off or fails, numbers still load; the briefing may be empty.
  • Channel split — name / BTC residual / wrapper shares of the move, labels only.
  • Decay — how the pad shrinks as hours to open fall (Friday-night vs now).
  • Stress · first to break — preset shocks (event→0, wrapper +1.5%, BTC residual −3%) and which dies first.
  • Sliders — local recompute of event / wrapper / BTC from the frozen JSON; no new write-up call.
  • Factor · size · receipt — same-hour premiums across the three names, whether size eats the band, and a copyable receipt.

Open Lattice

A Monday cash-open rehearsal grid. Rows are cash open (−3%…+3%); columns are BTC residual (−3% / 0 / +3%). Optional wrapper bump +1% on every cell. Load the last desk freeze or a sample, then run the lattice to see which cells still have room. Human decides — no orders.

Kill Board

Editable falsification criteria with traffic lights (green clear · amber watch · red firing). Load a freeze, refresh lights against current criteria, edit or lock rows in this browser. Criteria are yours to change; the board does not place orders.

Contagion Atlas

Shock one name and see whether stress stays local or hits NVDA, TSLA, and AAPL together. Wrapper shocks apply to the source name only; BTC residual and event shocks are shared. Heat shows room / no room / unstable per name. Use last desk freeze or sample freezes.

Data quality

Every numeric field is tagged. Failed feeds are never dressed up as live.

  • Observed — printed from a live feed response.
  • Computed — derived from calendar or formula (e.g. hours to cash open).
  • Assumed — placeholder, heuristic, or intentional skip; desk still runs.
  • Failed (source_failed) — feed unreachable or empty.

Hours to cash open are always populated (computed, or assumed only if the ET lookup falls back). Event class stays “none” for math when the event feed fails; the tag still says failed.

How to use

  1. Open the desk. Leave or edit the thesis, style, and notional. Run desk.
  2. Read the data-quality strip first. Note observed vs assumed vs failed before trusting the band.
  3. Check status, lamp, band, and this thesis vs counter-thesis. Read the briefing only as a write-up from those numbers.
  4. Scan decay, stress first-to-break, factor, and whether size eats room. Copy the receipt if you want a snapshot.
  5. Move sliders for a local what-if; they do not re-freeze feeds or re-draft the write-up.
  6. Open Lattice to rehearse Monday opens; Kill Board to lock falsification lights; Atlas to test whether one shock spills across names.

Freeze · solve · brief in short: lock tagged inputs, draw the band and checks, then a write-up from the frozen table. Direction words are stripped. You make the call.

Limits

  • You decide. The desk never prints buy, sell, long, or short.
  • No orders. Research output only.
  • Failed feeds are labelled. They are never dressed up as live.
  • The briefing follows the solved numbers. It does not invent prices.
  • Published constants stay fixed: k = 0.4 · stress pad default = 0.8%.
  • Universe is NVDA, TSLA, AAPL rToken / wrapper focus.

FAQ

Does it trade for me?

No. The desk never places orders. Research output only; you decide.

Why observed vs assumed?

Observed means a live feed returned the figure. Assumed means a placeholder, heuristic, or intentional skip so the desk can still run. Treat assumed lines as labelled uncertainty, not as quiet live quotes.

What is rToken / wrapper here?

The crypto-side leg that tracks the US name when cash is closed — the wrapper last used against cash last to form premium. Cash and wrapper are separate legs; if the wrapper feed fails, the desk may fall back to an assumed premium and will say so.

What if a feed fails?

That field is tagged failed (or assumed if a labelled fallback is used). The numeric desk still returns. Nothing is painted as observed when the source did not answer.

Sample vs live freeze?

Lattice, Kill Board, and Atlas can load the last desk freeze from this browser or a built-in sample freeze. Sample is for rehearsal when you have not run the desk yet; it is labelled sample.

Why no buy / sell?

The product is a decision aid, not an order ticket. Status and lamp describe room and alignment; they do not encode a side. Direction words are stripped from text outputs.

What is “hours to cash open”?

Computed time until the next US regular cash open (ET calendar). It drives decay of the stress pad and the clock on the desk. If the ET path fails, a fallback may be assumed and tagged.

What does event source_failed mean?

The event / calendar feed timed out or errored. Math may still use event class none; the tag tells you the quiet calendar was not verified. Assumed on events means no dated match, not “none exists.”

Can I edit kill criteria?

Yes. On the Kill Board you can edit and lock criteria in this browser, then refresh lights against a loaded freeze. Edits stay local to the browser.

Where does the briefing come from?

A write-up from the frozen table (symbol, legs, premium, residual, event, hours, band, first-to-break, factor, lamp). It does not invent prices. If the writer is unavailable, the briefing is empty and the numbers remain.
